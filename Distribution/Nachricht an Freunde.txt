— English —

Hey! I made you a mixtape 🎧

To listen, you’ll need the Mixtape app once (Mac, macOS 14.4 or later):
https://mixtape.toyko.online

Download it there, open the file, drag Mixtape into Applications and follow the “Read me first” guide. macOS shows a warning the first time (“Apple could not verify …”). That only means the app isn’t signed by Apple (it costs 99 USD a year) – there’s no malware in it. The code is public on GitHub, anyone can check it. No account, no tracking – your recordings stay on your Mac. Opening it takes a minute.

Then just double-click the attached .mixtape file. You can record something onto it yourself and send the cassette back to me.
